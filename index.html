Locally Owned

Since 1986, Sleepy Fox Coffee Shop has been your neighborhood spot to slow down, grab a fresh cup, and catch up with friends. Locally owned and fiercely independent for over forty years, we pride ourselves on small-batch roasts, warm hospitality, and a cozy atmosphere that still carries that classic eighties charm. Whether you are stopping in for your morning commute or settling into a corner booth for the afternoon, there is always a fresh pot waiting for you.

Award Winning

Sleepy Fox Coffee Shop is an award-winning specialty coffee roaster and café known for precision brewing and warm hospitality. Winner of the 2010 Best Cafe Award, we serve single-origin pour-overs, velvety espresso drinks, and house-made seasonal syrups alongside fresh local pastries. Drop in for a world-class cup or grab a bag of our signature beans to brew at home.
